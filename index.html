About Me

I'm Mochammad Ijdin Rozaqi, an Ocean Engineering graduate from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (GPA 3.68/4.00). My path started as an Intern Surveyor at Biro Klasifikasi Indonesia, inspecting ships during docking and verifying material and welding documentation — now I work as a Marine Engineer at PT. ZEE Engineering Indonesia, running mooring, rigging, and structural analysis across offshore projects.

My thesis analyzed the design and strength of vertical turbine reservoir structures for energy utilization in open canals. Safety runs through everything I do — I hold the AK3 Umum certification and more technical credentials.

WHY I SAIL

"A structure doesn't care how confident your calculation was — only whether it holds."

That's the standard I hold my own work to. I'm a recent Ocean Engineering graduate driven by curiosity and a genuine appetite for learning — eager to bring fresh eyes and real solutions to every team I join, and excited by the challenge of thriving in a fast-moving professional environment.

My path so far has taken me from surveying ships against classification rules in Indonesia to designing subsea pipeline and marine structures in the United States — two very different vantage points on the same question: will this hold up in the real world? I plan to keep chasing that question.
